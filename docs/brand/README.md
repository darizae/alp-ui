# Alp-ui — original logo asset bundle

**All assets are derived from the original, approved Alp-ui logo (`source/alp-ui-original.png`).** No new mascot, wordmark or geometry was generated.

## Contents

- `source/alp-ui-original.png` — exact original 1254 × 1254 PNG, unchanged.
- `on-background/lockup-original-on-white-exact-crop.png` — pixel-for-pixel crop of source, including the original white background.
- `on-background/icon-original-on-white-exact-crop.png` — same source crop for the icon, preserving its original white background.
- `transparent/lockup-original.png` — full logo with white background removed.
- `transparent/lockup-inverse.png` — same icon and wordmark shapes; dark Alp text recolored ivory for dark surfaces, emerald `-ui` unchanged.
- `transparent/icon-original.png` — icon-only, transparent outside the original rounded-square tile.
- `transparent/wordmark-original.png`, `transparent/wordmark-inverse.png` — optional text-only compositions.
- `on-background/lockup-original-on-white.png`, `on-background/lockup-inverse-on-navy.png` — preview/use-ready solid-background composites.
- `icons/` — downscaled PNG app icons and a multi-size Windows `.ico` from the same original image.
- `previews/` — visual comparison on light and dark backgrounds.

## Colors

- Alpaca Light primary: `#059669` emerald (brand theme reference).
- Alpaca Light background: `#F8FAFC` slate-white.
- Dark canvas: `#0F172A` slate navy.
- Inverse text: approximately `#F1F5F9`.

## Provenance and limits

The original logo is a raster PNG, not a vector/SVG master. Transparent variants use pixel-derived masks to remove the white paper, with minimal edge decontamination; very thin edge pixels may differ slightly from the original. The exact-crop PNGs are fully faithful source crops. No logo typeface or illustration has been re-created. If later vector artwork is needed, recreate it as a separate design job and compare against the original.
